How this page handles your keys
  • It comes from the board itself, over a hotspot only this phone could join, with a password that was shown on the board's screen.
  • It can't load or send anything anywhere except back to the board.
  • It never stores a value; each field empties after Save. The board answers only "saved", "missing" or "cleared" and a length.
  • Wi-Fi changes take effect when you tap Done, so this page isn't cut off halfway.
  • The hotspot switches itself off after 5 minutes.
