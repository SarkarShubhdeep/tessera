How this page handles your keys
  • It reaches the board only through the USB cable (Chrome's Web Serial). Nothing goes over Wi-Fi or the internet.
  • It can't load or send anything on the network: its Content Security Policy blocks every request.
  • It never stores a value. Each field is emptied after Save.
  • The board replies only "saved", "missing" or "cleared" and a length, never the value. Time zone and place too: they say roughly where you live.
  • Close screen or any other program using the board's port first.
